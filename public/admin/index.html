How the QR stays safe

Things worth knowing before you print a thousand cards:

  • The QR holds a random link, not the member's data — nobody can build a matching QR without access to this panel.
  • Scans always show the live record, so editing a name or photo updates every card instantly.
  • Revoke kills a card immediately; Reissue QR makes a new QR and voids all old printed copies.
  • The printed code (e.g. VR-4F2A-91C3) is signed, so it can also be checked by eye against the screen.
