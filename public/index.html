QR ID

Issue member identity cards whose QR code proves the card is genuine and shows the member's full record when scanned by any phone camera. Built for an organization register: political party, association, club, company or cooperative.

Open the admin panel

QR you cannot forge

The printed code is a random 128-bit link to this server — not the member's data — so nobody can manufacture a matching card without access to the panel.

Always up to date

Scanned pages read the live record. Fix a phone number or photo once and every card in circulation shows the new information.

Revoke a lost card

Mark a member revoked and the scan page refuses the card immediately. Reissue the QR to void every old printed copy.

Print-ready

Card backs come out at true ID-card size (85.6 × 54 mm) and can be printed as individual cards or as A4 sheets of eight.
